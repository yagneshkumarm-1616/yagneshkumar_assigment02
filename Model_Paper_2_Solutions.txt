Q5. Two dice are thrown. What is probability of getting sum = 7?
Topic: Probability - Compound | Difficulty: Medium
Category: Quantitative Aptitude

Options:
  ✓ A) 1/6
    B) 1/12
    C) 1/18
    D) 1/36

Correct Answer: 1/6

Explanation:
Favorable outcomes: (1,6), (2,5), (3,4), (4,3), (5,2), (6,1) = 6
Total outcomes = 36
P(sum=7) = 6/36 = 1/6

Q31. A sum becomes 4/3 of itself in 5 years at simple interest. What is rate of interest?
Topic: Simple Interest - Advanced | Difficulty: Hard
Category: Quantitative Aptitude

Options:
  ✓ A) 6.67%
    B) 10%
    C) 20%
    D) 25%

Correct Answer: 6.67%

Explanation:
SI = 4/3 P - P = 1/3 P
SI = P×R×T/100
1/3 P = P×R×5/100
R = 100/15 = 6.67%

Q10. Two items are sold at equal selling price. One at 20% profit, other at 20% loss. What is net loss%?

Category: Quantitative Aptitude

Options:
  ✓ A) 4%
    B) 5%
    C) 10%
    D) 20%

Correct Answer: 4%

Explanation:
Let SP = 100 each, CP1 = 100/1.2 = 83.33, CP2 = 100/0.8 = 125
Total CP = 208.33, Total SP = 200
Loss% = 8.33/208.33 × 100 = 4%
